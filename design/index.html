01 / Mouse

Aim quickly. Refine with touch.

Motion covers distance. Touch takes priority at ¼× or ⅛× gain; dragging has an explicit release state.

02 / Keyboard

Select first. Send when ready.

Motion, touch and spoken letters share one preview. Confirm Alpha → A before sending, without Enter.

03 / Audio

Independent first. Concurrent later.

Voice and music use different profiles. Qualify same-host concurrency before enabling it; multi-host operation is separate.
